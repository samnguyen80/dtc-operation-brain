# YouTube Sources for DTC and Ecommerce Operations

There is no single “Chase of operations” covering the whole DTC stack deeply. The best source mix is:

1. An operator-led channel for company-level trade-offs.
2. Specialist channels for inventory, supply chain/fulfillment, returns/CX, and finance.
3. Vendor channels as tactical sources, with commercial bias explicitly marked.

Channel and video metadata below were checked on 2026-08-29. Vendor case-study results are **claimed** and must not be treated as benchmarks.

## Recommended ingest set

If only four sources are ingested first:

1. **Operators Podcast** — operator governance, inventory, cash, organization, 3PL/owned-warehouse decisions.
2. **eCom Logistics Podcast** — 3PL, fulfillment, reverse logistics, and supply-chain systems.
3. **SCMDOJO** — neutral foundations for demand planning, inventory, and S&OP; not DTC-specific.
4. **LedgerGurus** — ecommerce accounting, COGS, inventory, cash flow, reconciliation, and tax.

Add **Davie Fogarty** as the most accessible end-to-end DTC operator overview.

## What not to ingest as operations doctrine

- Channels where operations means only ad automation, store setup, or dropshipping order forwarding.
- Tool demos without a transferable process, control, or metric.
- Universal numerical benchmarks with no category, promise, volume, or SLA context.
- Case-study outcomes without a **claimed** label.
- Legal/tax videos used as authority without checking current government sources.
