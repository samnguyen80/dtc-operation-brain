# DTC Operations knowledge

An evidence-backed operating system for founders and operators of product-based DTC and ecommerce brands. Open this `knowledge/` directory as an Obsidian vault.

Growth creates demand. Operations converts that demand into an order that is sourced, delivered, supported, reconciled, and profitable without starving the next inventory cycle of cash.

## Guardrails

- Revenue is only a maturity proxy. SKU count, channels, nodes, lead time, product regulation, and systems that can mutate inventory usually explain complexity better.
- A benchmark is not a control limit. Derive targets from the customer promise, brand baseline, category economics, and contractual SLA.
- Treat case-study figures as **[claimed]** and software recommendations from vendor channels as potentially biased.
- Legal, tax, customs, and product-compliance notes are research pointers, not professional advice; recheck the current rule and jurisdiction.
