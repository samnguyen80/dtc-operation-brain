# DTC Operations glossary

| Term | Operational meaning |
|---|---|
| **3PL** | Third-party logistics provider handling some combination of receiving, storage, pick/pack, shipping, and returns. |
| **ABC analysis** | Segmentation by economic importance, often placing the few SKUs driving most value in A. It does not capture demand variability by itself. |
| **ASN** | Advance shipment notice sent before inbound goods arrive, describing expected shipment contents. |
| **ATP** | Available to promise: inventory the business can responsibly commit to customers after reservations and constraints. |
| **BOM** | Bill of materials. For ecommerce it also maps bundles/kits to component SKUs. |
| **CAPA** | Corrective and preventive action: fix the current defect and remove the cause of recurrence. |
| **CCC** | Cash conversion cycle: `DIO + DSO - DPO`; an operating measure of how long cash is tied up. |
| **COGS** | Cost of goods sold recognized for units sold; it is not the same as cash paid for inventory. |
| **Contribution margin** | Revenue less a clearly defined set of variable costs. Always state which costs are included. |
| **Cycle count** | Recurring count of a subset of inventory to detect and correct record variance without a full shutdown. |
| **DIO** | Days inventory outstanding: approximate days that inventory remains tied up before sale. |
| **Dock-to-stock** | Time from an inbound shipment arriving at the facility until sellable units are received and available. |
| **DPO** | Days payable outstanding: approximate time the company takes to pay suppliers. |
| **DSO** | Days sales outstanding: approximate time from sale to cash collection. Payment processors can create timing/reserve effects even in ecommerce. |
| **ERP** | Enterprise resource planning system coordinating transactions and records across inventory, purchasing, fulfillment, and finance. |
| **Forecast bias** | Persistent over-forecasting or under-forecasting; different from average absolute error. |
| **Golden sample** | Approved physical reference against which production quality is judged. |
| **IMS** | Inventory management system. Responsibilities must be explicit when store, IMS, WMS, and ERP coexist. |
| **Inventory position** | `On hand + on order - committed/backordered`, adjusted to match the brand's status rules. |
| **Landed cost** | Product cost plus attributable freight, duty, insurance, brokerage, and other costs needed to bring inventory to its usable location. |
| **Lead time** | Time from a replenishment decision or PO to inventory becoming available; variability matters as much as the average. |
| **MOQ** | Minimum order quantity imposed by a supplier or production process. |
| **OMS** | Order management system coordinating orders, allocation, routing, holds, cancellations, and statuses. |
| **Open-to-buy** | A purchasing budget that links planned sales and ending inventory to beginning and already ordered inventory. |
| **OTIF** | On time in full: whether the supplier or logistics partner delivered the agreed quantity by the agreed time. |
| **RACI** | Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed. Use it to clarify ownership, not to replace decision rules. |
| **Reorder point** | Inventory position at which replenishment should be triggered, typically lead-time demand plus safety stock. |
| **RMA** | Return merchandise authorization and its associated return record/workflow. |
| **Safety stock** | Inventory buffer protecting against demand and lead-time uncertainty; it is a policy choice, not a fixed number of days. |
| **Sell-through** | Units sold as a share of units available/received for a defined cohort and period. State the denominator. |
| **SLA** | Service-level agreement defining a measurable promise, method, exclusions, reporting, and remedy/escalation. |
| **S&OP** | Sales and operations planning: cross-functional agreement on demand, supply, inventory, capacity, and financial implications. |
| **SKU master** | Governed record for SKU identifiers and operational attributes such as dimensions, cost, status, bundle relation, supplier, and location rules. |
| **WISMO** | “Where is my order?” contacts; often a symptom of delivery uncertainty or weak proactive communication. |
| **WMS** | Warehouse management system controlling warehouse tasks, locations, movements, and fulfillment execution. |
