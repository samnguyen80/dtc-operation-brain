# CX as an operations sensor

Customer support is both a resolution function and an early-warning sensor. Ticket volume alone is not insight; structured causes connected to orders, SKU/lot, shipment, and outcome are.

## Feedback loop

```text
contact → reason and root-cause code → resolution and cost
→ weekly pattern review → owner/action in product, PDP, supplier,
warehouse, carrier, policy, payment, or growth → verify recurrence falls
```

Separate the customer's stated reason from the operational root cause. “Where is my order?” may originate in receiving delay, allocation, warehouse backlog, carrier exception, or weak notification.

Track contact rate per order, reason mix, aged backlog, resolution time, reopen/escalation, refund lag, retention outcome, and recurrence after corrective action. Agent incentives should not reward fast closure at the cost of repeat contacts or avoidable refunds.
