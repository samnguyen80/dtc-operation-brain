# Supplier quality gate

Quality control should prevent a nonconforming batch from becoming customer tickets, returns, reviews, and cash loss.

## Minimum gate

- Written product specification/tech pack and approved golden sample.
- PO acceptance criteria and controlled approval of material/process changes.
- Risk-based pre-production, in-process, and/or pre-shipment checks.
- Lot/batch traceability from supplier to customer and return reason.
- Release/rework/reject decision with an accountable owner.
- CAPA for repeat defects and supplier performance review.

Unit price is incomplete. Compare landed, defect-adjusted, delay-adjusted, and working-capital cost. Inspection sampling standards and thresholds depend on category and risk; do not copy a vendor benchmark without current specialist review.
