# Contribution margin and cash conversion cycle

Revenue and gross margin do not show whether the next inventory cycle is profitable and fundable. Use a consistent contribution ladder and a cash-timing view.

```text
Net revenue
- landed COGS
- payment fees
- fulfillment, packaging, and shipping subsidy
- variable support, return, refund, and dispute leakage
- acquisition cost where relevant
= defined contribution margin
```

CM1/CM2/CM3 naming varies. Publish the inclusion rules and reconcile them to the books.

```text
Cash conversion cycle = DIO + DSO - DPO
```

Pair this with a rolling 13-week cash forecast and PO calendar. Review margin by SKU/channel/cohort and cash by payment date/scenario. Profit can coexist with a cash shortage because inventory deposits, production, freight, and duty precede sales and payouts.
