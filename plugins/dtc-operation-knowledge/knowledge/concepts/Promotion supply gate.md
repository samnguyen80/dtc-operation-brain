# Promotion supply gate

A promotion or spend increase is an operational commitment, not only a marketing decision. Before launch, growth and operations agree on demand scenarios, inventory allocation, fulfillment capacity, cash exposure, and intervention thresholds.

## Gate

1. Baseline demand and incremental lift assumptions by hero SKU.
2. Sellable inventory, inbound confidence, and allocation by channel.
3. Warehouse/3PL labor, carrier, and cut-off capacity.
4. CX, return, fraud, and refund capacity.
5. Contribution and cash downside.
6. Trigger to reduce spend, change offer, substitute, backorder, or stop.

After the event, replace assumptions with actuals and feed the variance into the next forecast. Do not celebrate revenue while separately discovering stockouts, split shipments, backlog, or cash damage.
