# Operating cadence and decision rights

Operations scales when repeated decisions leave the founder's head and enter a controlled rhythm.

## A complete operating rule

```text
trigger + input + owner + decision right + procedure
+ control/metric + threshold + escalation + output/evidence + backup
```

An SOP without an owner is documentation. An owner without a decision right becomes an escalation router. A KPI without a threshold becomes reporting.

## Cadence

- Daily: close aged customer, order, inventory, delivery, integration, and cash exceptions.
- Weekly: decide on SKU risk, POs, growth constraints, supplier/3PL issues, returns causes, and cash.
- Monthly: S&OP, financial close, reserves, capacity, and scorecards.
- Quarterly: contracts, network/supplier strategy, scenario tests, access, SOP health, and business continuity.

Measure repeat exceptions, decision latency, founder escalations, corrective-action closure, SLA adherence, and single-person dependencies. Automate after the source of truth and manual decision are reliable.
