# 3PL governance

A 3PL transfers execution, not accountability. Govern it as a measured extension of the brand.

## Before contracting

Use the real order profile: SKU count, dimensions, bundles/kitting, order lines, seasonality, returns, channels, destinations, and peak. Normalize the rate card and model receiving, storage, pick/pack, packaging, postage, projects, returns, minimums, and surcharges.

## Operating contract

- Define cutoffs, on-time ship, accuracy, receiving, inventory, and returns metrics precisely.
- Require ASN/process compliance, cycle counts, data access, escalation, root-cause reports, and remedies.
- Agree peak capacity, forecast cadence, change control, and implementation tests.
- Preserve an exit plan, inventory transfer procedure, and data ownership.

Review exceptions daily, reconcile weekly, and hold business reviews against trends and corrective actions. The target is not a generic industry number; it is the promise and risk written into the brand's contract.
