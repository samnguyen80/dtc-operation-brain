# Open-to-buy and PO cash calendar

Open-to-buy constrains inventory purchasing to a planned sales and ending-inventory position:

```text
Open-to-buy = planned ending inventory + planned sales
              - beginning inventory - on-order inventory
```

The inventory plan must be paired with a cash calendar showing deposits, balance payments, freight, duty, receiving, and expected payouts. A PO can be economically sensible but impossible to fund at the required dates.

## Decision questions

- Which SKU and scenario justifies the commitment?
- When does cash leave and when is it expected to return?
- What is the cancel/defer/split/expedite decision date?
- Does the PO protect a hero SKU or deepen slow inventory?
- What downside remains if forecast or lead time misses?
