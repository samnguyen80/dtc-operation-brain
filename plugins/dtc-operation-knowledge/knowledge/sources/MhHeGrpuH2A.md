# Why eCommerce Sellers Need an Inventory Management System, Even with a 3PL

- **Channel:** LedgerGurus: Ecommerce Accounting Experts
- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/MhHeGrpuH2A
- **Date:** 2024-05-08 · 2:00

**Thesis:** A 3PL executes warehouse and fulfillment work, but it does not replace the brand's inventory control plane for purchasing, forecasting, channel availability, inventory valuation, and COGS.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- A 3PL commonly picks, packs, ships, and reports warehouse activity; those physical services do not make it the owner of end-to-end inventory planning or accounting.
- The missing layer is cross-channel available inventory, replenishment forecasting, purchasing, and the financial cost attached to units.
- An inventory management system should preserve state across the journey: supplier or in transit, warehouse shelf, allocated to an order, in transit to a customer, and sold.
- Historical sales and lead-time inputs can support purchase-order generation and reduce manual reorder decisions; software does not remove the need to validate those inputs.
- COGS automation matters because a warehouse quantity report alone cannot establish product profitability.
