# How Hemper Runs Subscription Boxes, Manufacturing & Multi-Brand Wholesale Operations

- **Channel:** Fulfil
- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/-L_-hYz8MZc
- **Date:** 2025-05-02 · 4:52

**Thesis:** Subscription, manufacturing, multi-brand wholesale, and fulfillment require shared batching, routing, production, purchasing, returns, and reporting; replacing paper handoffs with scan-based workflows can expose capacity and inventory truth.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- The business combines a subscription box, online retail, manufacturing, wholesale, and multiple brands. Its supply arm is said to produce roughly **100 million pre-roll cones per month** **[claimed]**.
- Before systemization, the team lacked visibility into available inventory, shipping timeliness, box production, receiving, and scale KPIs. Existing software lacked batching, routing, order splits, and subscription-box packing logic.
- Scan-based location sequencing and order batching replaced a largely paper/highlighter workflow. The wholesale operating team reportedly moved from about **six people** to **two coordinators plus one or two pickers**, while throughput rose from **10–15 to 40–50 orders per day** **[claimed]**.
- Sales-order-to-purchase-request-to-draft-PO work reportedly fell from about **12 hours per transaction** to under **15 minutes** **[claimed]**.
- The speaker reports a **24–48 hour** shipping promise; a large **1,200-item** order that once took **one to two weeks** was completed in at most **three days** **[claimed]**.
- Returns administration reportedly moved from multi-day lag to about **30 minutes per day** **[claimed]**.
- Forecasting and data-warehouse outputs support order-based subscription-box production rather than prebuilding batches of **1,000, 5,000, or 20,000 boxes** **[claimed]**; APIs push selected state to Google Sheets, Slack, and ClickUp.
