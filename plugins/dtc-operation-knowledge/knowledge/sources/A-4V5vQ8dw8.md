# 5 Levels of Supply Chain Planning

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/A-4V5vQ8dw8
- **Date:** 2017-05-21 · 6:08

**Thesis:** Planning must cascade from financial intent to cross-functional demand/supply consensus, then to master scheduling, material replenishment, and executable factory-line work.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- Annual profit planning sets revenue, product, headcount, CapEx, inventory-days, and turns targets.
- Monthly S&OP reconciles unconstrained commercial demand with supplier, production, labor, capacity, and delivery constraints to create a consensus plan.
- A biweekly master production schedule connects sales/marketing with manufacturing, purchasing, and engineering; it depends on accurate bills of materials and inventory.
- Weekly/daily material planning converts the master plan into MRP, reorder-point, min/max, or kanban actions.
- Factory scheduling must specify line-level work and measure work-order release and completion rather than merely pushing work orders to production.
