# 4 China Sourcing Secrets for DTC Brands

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/KrKfmWBoGSM
- **Date:** 2024-05-21 · 48:01
- **Channel:** EcommOps

**Thesis:** Professional China sourcing is a managed chain of due diligence, sampling, production control, QC, shipping, and supplier relationships; optimizing only unit price usually moves cost or risk elsewhere.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- Supplier discovery must distinguish a real factory from a trading company or a factory reselling another factory's product. The guest says his team may contact **30–50 companies** during diligence **[claimed]**; the durable point is to verify capability and counterparty identity before committing (6:04–10:07).
- Samples are comparative tests, not merely pass/fail objects. A cosmetic flaw may matter less than the supplier's engineering strength, ability to customize, and willingness to improve the product (7:04–10:07).
- QC needs a brand-owned checklist plus production visibility. The speakers recommend checkpoints while materials arrive and assembly/filling is underway, not only inspection after the run is complete; forcing speed can make the factory trade quality for timing (11:08–14:44).
- Relationship quality affects flexibility on production schedules, pricing, raw-material commitments, and payment terms. One example describes receiving net-15 terms after roughly a year and a half **[claimed]**; this is a relationship anecdote, not a universal supplier term (16:47–24:11).
- Negotiate against expected six-month volume and a smoother production schedule rather than buying one oversized batch solely to reach a price tier. Smaller, more frequent releases can reduce inventory cash exposure while giving the factory steadier work (22:50–27:35).
- Packaging is part of landed economics and customer experience: dimensions and weight affect freight and mailbox fit, while retail-oriented packaging may break or disappoint when shipped DTC (27:35–31:04).
- The episode argues for separating sourcing expertise from fulfillment expertise as brands scale. It says combined shipping agents have padded sourcing prices by **40–50%** in observed cases **[claimed]**; treat this as vendor testimony and independently bid both functions (32:17–36:04).
