# Long-Term Demand & Supply Planning for Ecommerce

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/wJAe9WUUNWE
- **Date:** 2024-09-19 · 1:16

**Thesis:** A long-term demand plan should provide an editable monthly revenue forecast that informs supplier commitments and target inventory investment while keeping the human planner in control.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- Forecast future monthly sales and revenue far enough ahead to support supplier negotiations and supply commitments.
- Use the plan to reduce avoidable inventory and warehouse cost, releasing cash for growth or other priorities.
- Treat automated forecasts as a proposal: planners must be able to override the output with known promotions, launches, constraints, or judgment.
