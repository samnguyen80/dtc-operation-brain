# 4 Easy Steps to Inventory Improvement

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/jxH1oEGP-iE
- **Date:** 2022-02-22 · 7:19

**Thesis:** Improve inventory by diagnosing availability and aging, creating a service and replenishment policy for every SKU segment, managing a small strategic KPI set, and only then selecting software that supports the required controls.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- Start with product availability by ABC class so the highest-demand or highest-value items receive the strongest service protection.
- Inspect excess, obsolete, and aging inventory by COGS value; the speaker uses more than six months as an excess-stock example and no movement for 12 months as dead stock [claimed], not universal cutoffs.
- Create a plan for every SKU by combining value and demand-frequency segmentation rather than applying one service-level or safety-stock rule across the catalog.
- Keep KPIs tied to supply-chain strategy: availability, delivery in full/on time, supplier in full/on time, stock turns, forecast accuracy, and gross-margin return on inventory.
- Choose inventory software from functional requirements such as low-stock alerts, multi-location balancing, forecasting, replenishment, container or network optimization, and reporting—not from vendor prominence.
