# Creating a Supply Plan

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/G4CDHSNxqqg
- **Date:** 2024-09-19 · 1:29

**Thesis:** Convert an approved demand plan into editable purchase recommendations that respect the product structure and supplier constraints before a human releases a PO.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- Demand-plan changes should propagate into recommended purchase quantities so the demand and supply views do not diverge silently.
- Recommended orders must account for bundles, supplier MOQ, and batch-size constraints; forecast demand alone is not executable supply.
- A planner should review and edit the recommendation before converting it to a draft PO and communicating it to the supplier.
