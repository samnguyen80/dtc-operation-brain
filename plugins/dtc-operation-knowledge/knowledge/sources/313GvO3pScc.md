# Complete E-commerce Operations Guide — Advanced Tutorial

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/313GvO3pScc
- **Date:** 2024-06-25 · 1:20:07

**Thesis:** Ecommerce operations is the coordinated system connecting product specification, suppliers, planning, purchase orders, freight, fulfillment, unit economics, and customer service; weak handoffs in any one area erase growth elsewhere.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- Define products with a technical pack—drawings, materials, measurements, colors, hardware, labels, packaging, and tolerances—so samples, production, QC, disputes, and supplier transitions reference the same specification.
- Use a critical path with every product-development step, dependency, owner, duration, and date. Inspect initial production runs against the specification before shipping.
- Forecast replenishment with clean in-stock history, seasonality, promotion, growth assumptions, supplier lead time, target stock cover, and safety stock; inventory excess and stockouts are both existential cash risks.
- Treat the PO as the cross-functional spine. Finance, inventory, marketing, CX, freight, and the 3PL need controlled visibility into quantity, SKU, price, shipment, ETA, and receipt changes.
- Calculate contribution from actual selling price less product, freight, duties, pick/pack, storage, outbound delivery, payment fees, and acquisition cost; keep sales tax treatment internally consistent.
- Select 3PLs through an RFP, facility inspection, barcode/integration review, reference checks, and explicit SLA. Audit inbound, storage, pick/pack, outbound carrier, and accessorial invoices monthly.
- CX output metrics—resolution time, touches, reviews, cost per ticket, and delivered-on-time—are lagging evidence of upstream product, planning, freight, and fulfillment quality.
