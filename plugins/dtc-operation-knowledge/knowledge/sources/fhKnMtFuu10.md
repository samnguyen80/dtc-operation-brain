# How Monos Scaled a 9-Figure Supply Chain Without Adding Headcount

- **Channel:** Fulfil
- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/fhKnMtFuu10
- **Date:** 2026-07-27 · 5:33

**Thesis:** A multi-region DTC supply chain needs inventory and process state outside 3PL exports and master spreadsheets; a suitable ERP can become the shared layer across procurement, fulfillment, reverse logistics, channels, and partners.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- Monos describes a supply-chain scope from procurement through fulfillment and reverse logistics across North America, the EU, UK, Australia, and emerging regions; it reports **nine-figure revenue** **[claimed]**.
- Before the ERP, inventory management depended on 3PL WMS exports combined in master Excel files, fragmenting the source of truth.
- The speaker reports an **18-month** surge in regions, channels, and supply-chain complexity while maintaining the same headcount and workload **[claimed]**.
- Partner readiness is treated as an operating capability: Amazon 1P/FBA and 3PL integrations can reduce the work and risk of entering a channel or market.
- The speaker says at least **80% of prospective major 3PLs** had heard of Fulfil **[claimed]**; this is a testimonial observation, not market-share evidence.
- Traditional ERP implementation is characterized as **six months to two years**, while Fulfil opportunities are described as taking **weeks rather than months** **[claimed]**.
- The case emphasizes iterative product change, a small hands-on implementation team, and alignment with a tech-forward operating culture.
