# How He Makes Millions Sourcing Products From China

- **Watch:** https://youtu.be/83BpzPYX-vM
- **Date:** 2024-12-20 · 16:55

**Thesis:** Professional sourcing reduces execution risk by making requirements unambiguous, maintaining vetted alternatives, and matching country/factory economics to raw materials, labor, tax, lead time, quality, and scale.

## Transcript-grounded takeaways

- Alibaba can surface adequate factories, but finding a best-fit factory requires repeated vetting, samples, category expertise, and direct knowledge of factory capability.
- Maintain qualified backup factories and let the incumbent know alternatives exist; this protects continuity and reduces the chance that performance deteriorates after dependence forms.
- Replace ambiguous instructions such as “centered” with dimensions, tolerances, drawings, tech packs, and explicit checkpoints; the speaker's agency uses an 80-step sourcing process [claimed].
- Samples and reference products should precede a meaningful production run; a bad run loses both inventory cash and market timing.
- Country choice is a portfolio decision across raw-material origin, labor, tax, manufacturing maturity, transport, lead time, and scale—not simply the lowest unit quote.
- Owning a factory is not a beginner move; it requires onsite operational depth and is most defensible when it fixes an enduring quality, R&D, speed, or cost constraint.
