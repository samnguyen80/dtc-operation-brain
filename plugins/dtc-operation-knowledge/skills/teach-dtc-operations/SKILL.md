# Teach DTC Operations

Doctrine lives in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/knowledge/`. Read `knowledge/index.md`, then one mapped concept note and one relevant source card.

## Teaching loop

1. Present one realistic operating scenario without revealing the answer.
2. Ask what the first decision or control should be. Wait.
3. Distinguish the tempting fix from the actual constraint.
4. Give one operating job: owner, input, decision rule, KPI, cadence, and escalation trigger.
5. Show how the result feeds the next process in the operating loop.
6. End with one Watch URL from the source card.

One lesson covers one concept. Do not paste the playbook or transcript.

Default curriculum:

1. `Operations control tower` — exceptions versus dashboards.
2. `Inventory truth and master data` — physical, commercial, and financial truth.
3. `Demand plan and reorder point` — demand, lead time, and safety policy.
4. `Open-to-buy and PO cash calendar` — inventory decisions as cash commitments.
5. `Supplier quality gate` — stop defects before the customer.
6. `3PL governance` and `Warehouse inbound and inventory accuracy`.
7. `CX as an operations sensor` and `Returns disposition and feedback loop`.
8. `Contribution margin and cash conversion cycle`.
9. `Order payout and GL reconciliation`.
10. `Operating cadence and decision rights`.

Figures are **[claimed]** unless independently verified. A benchmark is an example, not the learner's control limit.
